# Cookie Notice

## What cookies and storage are

Cookies are small entries a website can store in your browser; local storage entries behave similarly but persist per origin. Both can remember choices and — in less careful sites — track you. That second use is what this site avoids entirely.

## What we set today

- ltab-region — your chosen or auto-detected region, so headings, prices and the legal documents match your market.
- ltab-lab-step — your place in the Freedom Lab form, so a refresh does not destroy your answers.
- ltab-consent — your privacy choice for this site, so we stop asking once you have answered.
- ltab-jurisdiction — inside the Legal Center only: which document variant (Europe or UK) you picked when both apply to your region.
All four are functional storage: no identifiers, no expiry tricks, no third parties reading them. Deleting them costs nothing except your place in a form.

## What we do not set

No advertising or retargeting pixels. No social-platform trackers. No third-party analytics — including Google Analytics, today. No web fonts fetched from Google: Bricolage Grotesque, Manrope and JetBrains Mono are served from this site itself, so no font vendor sees your visits.

Regimes differ on when consent is needed. The banner link reopens the choice; deleting the ltab-consent storage entry does the same. Browser settings always govern cookies your browser holds, whatever any site says. To remove everything this site set, use your browser’s "clear site data" for this origin, or the banner’s reset.
