# Data Collection Declaration
_The short version you get when it matters most — before you send anything · New Zealand · Effective 6 October 2026_

This is the at-the-form declaration: exactly what a submission contains, exactly why it is needed, who receives it and what happens next. It is deliberately short; the Privacy Policy and the section for your region carry the law. If a field is unclear, ask hello@ltab.ai before you decide.


## We are telling you this before you send

Every form on this site shows a note — "By sending, you agree we store these details to reply; the region link opens the full declaration" — and this document is what opens. Sending is consent to what is described here; not sending costs you nothing.

## Field by field

- Idea / product description — needed — why: without it there is nothing to reply to — basis: your enquiry, and contract performance once services start.
- Needs, stage, timeline, budget — optional — why: route the enquiry to the right team and shape the first reply — basis: your instruction, or legitimate interests.
- Region — pre-filled from your time zone, changeable — why: currency, time-zone overlap notes and which legal documents apply — basis: legitimate interests.
- Name and email — needed — why: to reply — basis: your enquiry; marketing only with extra consent where required.

- UTM parameters and source page — auto — why: know which link or campaign earned the reply, so we do not ask you — basis: legitimate interests.
- QA intake extras (stack, platform, package, release date, access readiness, role, company, country) — needed or optional as marked — why: quote and schedule a test that fits your reality — basis: as marked; contract once agreed.

## Where the submission goes

Into a CRM webhook when the connection is live, and today, while it is finalised, only into the studio console — no third-party receives a copy at this stage. When the CRM connects it will be named here first, with its region of storage.

## Who reads it inside the studio

The lead, the testing lead or strategist for your enquiry, and the engineers assigned if your project starts. Sensitive content typo’d into a form is not kept: tell us in the reply thread and it is removed from the record.

## How long

The region section sets the exact rule; 24 months from last contact is the default, shorter if you ask us to delete, longer only where a signed contract or law requires it.

**Laws that apply here**

- **Privacy Act 2020** — Thirteen Information Privacy Principles (IPPs): lawful purpose, source and notification, collection manner, storage security, access, correction, accuracy, retention, use limits, disclosure limits, unique identifiers, and cross-border disclosure (IPP 12).
- **Privacy Amendment Act 2024** — Strengthened penalties and compliance order powers; disclosure of intimate images without consent is a criminal offence under the Act.
- **Spam Act 2007** — Commercial electronic messages need consent, accurate sender information and a working unsubscribe.

**Regulator:** Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC). (https://www.privacy.org.nz/your-rights/making-a-complaint/)

**Your rights**

- Ask for access to your personal information — we answer within 20 working days
- Ask for a correction, and if we decline, have the disputed note attached
- Withdraw any consent and expect collection to stop for anything not legally required
- Expect IPP 12 discipline on any offshore disclosure
- Complain to the OPC if we fall short; a notifiable breach must reach them within 72 hours of us assessing it as notifiable

**Timing:** Access requests: 20 working days as the Act provides.

**Consent mode:** Tells you first (notice)

**Data leaving your country:** Under IPP 12 we disclose offshore only if the recipient is subject to comparable safeguards — our India engineering hub is bound by contract to the IPP-equivalent standard.

**How long we keep it:** Enquiries: 24 months from our last contact. Contracted work: the retention your contract sets, or as long as business records law requires.
