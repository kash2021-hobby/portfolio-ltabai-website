## Region specifics — undefined

**Laws that apply here**

- **UK GDPR** — Regulation (EU) 2016/679 as retained and amended in UK law, applied by the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
- **Data Protection Act 2018** — The UK’s own completion of the GDPR framework, including lawful-basis exemptions and law-enforcement processing.
- **PECR** — The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations: prior consent for non-essential cookies and rules for electronic marketing, including the "soft opt-in" for details collected during a sale.
- **International transfers** — From 21 March 2022 UK law allows the International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU SCCs plus a transfer risk assessment — that is what our India processing uses.

**Regulator:** Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). (https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/)

**Your rights**

- Get a copy of your personal data (access)
- Have inaccurate data corrected
- Have your data erased where the law allows
- Restrict processing while we check a dispute
- Get your data in a portable, machine-readable format
- Object to processing based on our legitimate interests
- Object at any time to direct marketing — we stop immediately
- Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything already lawfully done
- Not be subject to a decision made about you solely by automated means

**Timing:** We answer within one month, free of charge, and tell you within the same month if we need more time.

**Consent mode:** Asks first (prior opt-in consent)

**Data leaving your country:** Data is processed in our India engineering hub under the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (or the IDTA) with a transfer risk assessment. Ask us for a copy.

**How long we keep it:** Enquiries: 24 months from our last contact. Contracted work: the retention your contract sets. Consent records: three years, or until you withdraw, whichever is later.
