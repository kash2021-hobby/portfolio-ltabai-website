In plain English

What is QA?

QA means testing your software in a planned way — so the problems are found by us, not by your customers, your client or your staff. The important ones get fixed before you launch, hand over or go live.

Types of testing

The testing your
product needs.

Some problems need one kind of testing. Some need another. Here are the five we run most — in plain words, and what each one saves you from.

01

Manual testing

A real person uses your app like a customer would: sign up, search, book, pay. They click, type and try to break it.

Why it mattersFinds the confusing and broken things no robot can see — before a customer does.
02

Compatibility testing

We open your product on different phones, tablets, browsers and screen sizes, and check it works and looks right everywhere.

Why it mattersStops the "but it works on my laptop" surprise when a customer opens it on a phone.
03

Performance testing

We test your product under pressure: heavy traffic, slow internet, long lists. Does it keep up?

Why it mattersA slow page loses customers. You learn this before launch day, not after.
04

Automation testing

We write small robots that re-check the important parts every time you change something. Fast, and they never get bored.

Why it mattersQuick checks on the parts that must never break. No paying someone to click the same thing every week.
05

Penetration testing

We try to break into your own system on purpose — with your written permission — to find the weak spots first.

Why it mattersYou see the holes while they're still harmless. Specialists only, on a separate scope.
06

AI feature testing

We test the AI inside your app like a skeptic: made-up answers, trick questions, unsafe replies, leaked secrets.

Why it mattersAI fails in new ways. You see them before your customers do.
Also part of the mix

Regression testing

You changed one thing. We re-check the others, so nothing older quietly breaks.

Functional testing

Does every button do what it promises — every time, in every situation?

Security testing

We look for common weak spots: weak passwords, exposed data, doors that should be locked.

API testing

We check the pipes behind your app — the parts that move data around.

Good to know

Questions,
answered clearly.

What will you actually test?

Whatever your product needs: manual, compatibility, performance, automation, penetration — plus regression, functional, security and API checks. First we agree the list together: your features, your customer journeys, sign-up, forms, checkout, devices, and the pipes behind the scenes.

Which testing types should I choose?

You don't have to decide alone. Tell us what you ship and when it goes live. We'll suggest the smallest useful set. Most first projects start with manual and compatibility testing, then add more once the product is stable.

What do we need to provide?

A working test link. Authorised access. Test data (or permission to create it). The devices you support. Someone who can answer questions. Someone who owns the fixes. If access is incomplete, the test window moves — we'll tell you early.

Can you work under our agency brand?

Yes. First we agree confidentiality, report format, client contact and escalation rules. The work still records its scope and decisions clearly — nothing hidden from you.

Do you test a live website?

We start on a test version of your site. Live testing only with written permission, agreed limits, and a contact who can respond if something unexpected happens.

Is penetration testing included?

Not by default. Our standard tests can note security observations in scope, but a real penetration test is separate specialist work, with written permission and its own price. We tell you when you need it — and when you don't.

Can you guarantee zero problems?

No. And be careful with anyone who says yes. We report what we found in the agreed scope and name the risk that remains. No finite review can promise complete coverage.

How are the prices set?

The prices shown are indicative USD starting points. The final quote names the product, pages, devices, test window, deliverables and who does what. Outside the US, we quote in your currency.

How fast can you start?

Usually two to five working days after the scope is agreed. Hard launch date? Say so in the form — we'll tell you honestly if we can meet it.
