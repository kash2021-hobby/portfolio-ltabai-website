In plain English

What is QA?

QA means quality assurance: checking software in a planned way so you find the problems before your customers, your client or your staff do. LTAB gives you an independent check, so the important issues are fixed before you launch, hand over or go live.

Types of testing

The testing your
product needs.

Different problems need different kinds of testing. Here are the ones we run most often, in plain words, and what each one saves you from.

01

Manual testing

A real person uses your website or app the way a customer would: signing up, searching, booking, paying. They click, type, and try to break it.

Why it mattersCatches the confusing and broken things a script cannot see, before a customer finds them.
02

Compatibility testing

We open your product on different browsers, phones, tablets and screen sizes, and check it looks and works the same everywhere.

Why it mattersStops the "it works on my laptop" surprise when a customer opens it on an iPhone or an older Android.
03

Performance testing

We check how your product behaves under pressure: slow pages, heavy traffic, long lists, and visitors on a weak connection.

Why it mattersA slow page loses customers. You find out how it holds up before launch day, not during it.
04

Automation testing

We write scripts that repeat the same checks automatically, every single time you change something.

Why it mattersFast, repeatable checks on the parts that must never break, without paying for the same click-through by hand each week.
05

Penetration testing

An authorised attempt to break into your own system on purpose, to find the weak spots before someone else does.

Why it mattersShows you the holes while they are still harmless. Run by specialists, on a separate scope and written permission.
Also part of the mix

Regression testing

After a change, we re-check the older features to make sure nothing that used to work has quietly broken.

Functional testing

Does every feature do exactly what it is supposed to do, in every situation it will meet in real life?

Security testing

We look for the common weaknesses: weak logins, exposed data, and easy ways in that should not be there.

API testing

We check the behind-the-scenes connections that pass data between your app, your database and other systems.

What is a testing lab?

A testing lab is simply the controlled setup where we run your product the way it will be used in the real world. It holds the browsers, phones and tablets your customers actually own, test accounts for each type of user, realistic sample data, and one place to record every issue with its evidence. Nothing is guesswork, and your live site is never at risk while we work.

Good to know

Questions,
answered clearly.

What will you actually test?

Any mix of manual, compatibility, performance, automation and penetration testing, plus regression, functional, security and API checks. In practice we agree the list first: your features, your customer journeys, sign-in, forms, checkout or booking, browser and device behaviour, and the connections behind the scenes.

Which testing types should I choose?

You do not have to decide alone. Tell us what you are shipping and when it goes live, and we will suggest the smallest useful set. Most first projects start with manual and compatibility testing, then add performance or automation once the product is stable.

What do we need to provide?

A usable website or app build, authorised access, test data (or permission to create it), key requirements or known limits, the devices you support, someone who can answer questions, and a person who owns the fixes. Incomplete access moves the test window.

Can you work under our agency brand?

Yes, once confidentiality, the report format, client contact and escalation rules are agreed. The work still records its scope, issues and decisions clearly, so nothing is hidden from you.

Do you test a live website?

We normally start in a test or staging environment. We only test live with written permission, agreed limits, and a contact who can respond if something unexpected happens.

Is penetration testing included?

Not by default. Our standard testing can note security observations inside the agreed scope, but a real penetration test is separate specialist work with its own written permission and its own price. We will tell you when it is genuinely needed and when it is not.

Can you guarantee zero problems?

No, and you should be careful with anyone who says yes. We report the evidence found inside the agreed scope, with the remaining risk named honestly. No finite review can promise complete coverage.

How are the prices set?

The prices shown are indicative USD starting points. The final quote names the product, the pages or journeys, the devices, the test window, the deliverables, your responsibilities, and the rules for changes. If you are outside the United States we will quote in your currency.

How fast can you start?

Usually within two to five working days of the scope being agreed. If you are on a hard launch date, say so in the form and we will tell you honestly whether we can meet it.
