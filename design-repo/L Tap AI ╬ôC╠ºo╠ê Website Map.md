## 2. Signature concept: "The Shape of Freedom"

The whole site centres on one **3D object, an orange form that can be shaped** (soft, clay-like, made of light). It stands for "your idea, any shape."

- **Hero:** visitors drag, pinch, or tilt (gyroscope on phones) to reshape it. No two visitors end up with the same shape.
- **As you scroll:** the same object changes shape for each section. It turns into a website wireframe, an e-commerce cart, an agent network, a workflow pipeline, and finally the visitor's own idea.
- **Story:** the object never becomes "a template." It always stays loose and moving, which is the freedom message shown without words.

Having one hero object keeps the site from feeling like a random 3D demo, and keeps performance under control.

## 6. Imagine Lab (signature feature)

The freedom message as a working tool, and your best lead generator.

1. Visitor types their idea in plain words ("An app for my bakery that takes WhatsApp orders and tracks delivery").
2. AI turns it into a **live brief**: suggested modules, AI agents involved, automations, platforms (web, mobile, e-commerce), and a rough timeline and budget range.
3. The 3D form **becomes their idea**: modules appear as connected nodes they can add or remove.
4. Visitor tweaks it (no forms, just chips and toggles), then clicks **"Send to L Tap AI"**. This books a consultation with the brief already attached.

Works on mobile as a step-by-step flow.

## 10. Visual direction (draft)

- **Colours:** your logo orange as the hero colour, deep black/ink, warm off-white. Plus a small range of **light-based secondary colours** (e.g. magenta, electric blue, lime) that only show up in 3D lighting and gradients on the object, so the UI stays clean and the 3D carries the colour.
- **Type:** a very bold display typeface for statements, a clean sans for UI. Big, confident, few words.
- **Mood:** bold and playful but premium. Large empty space around one strong 3D object.
- **Note:** the design system attached to this project (Serviceflow CRM) belongs to a different brand. For L Tap AI I'd build a new mini system from your logo.

## 11. Technical and UX principles

- **3D stack:** WebGL (Three.js / React Three Fiber) with one shared scene that morphs, rather than many separate scenes.
- **Mobile-first 3D:** lighter models, touch + gyroscope controls, lower pixel ratio, still image fallback on low-power devices.
- **Accessibility:** respects "reduced motion" with a calm static version, keyboard navigation, readable contrast on top of 3D.
- **Performance budget:** first content in under 2.5s, 3D loads after the headline is visible.
- **SEO:** regional URLs (/us, /eu, /au, /ae) with hreflang, service pages, blog.
- **Conversion tracking:** Imagine Lab briefs, consultation bookings, WhatsApp clicks, all tracked by region.
